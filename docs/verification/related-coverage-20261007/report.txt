Source change
-------------
packages/handrail_ai_client/lib/src/display_session.dart and src/session.dart:
* Preserve the initiating control revision separately from a successful context
  page's coverage. Mint an internal one-use receipt only for a fully merged,
  single-group, unpaged, non-deferred, untrimmed page ahead of that control.
* Consume it on the next refresh only after fresh authorized control AND a fresh,
  empty, fully consumed changes response at exactly the covered revision. No
  JSON-equality shortcut, revision floor, cached authorization, timer, scheduler,
  global cache, or TTL was added.
* Bind the receipt to the session/client, activation, generation, exact window
  and selection, workspace selection generation, window version, turn membership,
  related/approval epochs and observation/read counters. Pending display reads,
  navigation attempts, changed membership, partial changes, failures, deferred
  content and truncation disqualify it. Even fully loaded multi-group views are
  deliberately ineligible; a single group's head cannot certify all groups.
* Spend the receipt before awaiting control, including failures. Clear it on
  deactivation/disposal and further related reads. Host account/client lifecycle
  disposal remains required; this adds no authentication/policy lifetime API.
* Do not stamp an initiating head as hydrated when its related read was discarded.
  Reject late success/failure after a same-revision window replacement as well
  as the existing generation/activation checks. A discarded load-more success
  no longer clears a newer revocation error.
* Fresh control, changes, append CAS, exact-turn admission/start, reconciliation,
  authorization and Retry-After paths are retained.
